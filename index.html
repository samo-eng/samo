ABOUT SAMO

Multidisciplinary engineering backed by practical execution.

Established in 2016, SAMO for Engineering & General Contracting Services supports aviation, healthcare, hospitality, government, commercial, residential and infrastructure projects.

Our work covers mechanical and electrical engineering, HVAC, plumbing, firefighting, fire alarm and low-current systems, renewable energy, engineering design, BoQ preparation, testing, commissioning, operation and maintenance.
